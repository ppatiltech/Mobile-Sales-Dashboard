# 📱 Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive **Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI** to monitor mobile transactions, sales performance, quantity trends, pricing, brands, payment methods, customer ratings, cities, and mobile models.

The dashboard converts mobile sales transaction data into an interactive business intelligence solution that helps identify **top-performing brands, customer payment preferences, sales trends, product demand, and customer satisfaction patterns**.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

## 1. Overall Sales Performance

The dashboard records **291 transactions** and approximately **59M in total sales**, with an average displayed price of **40.98K**.

This indicates a relatively high average transaction value within the analyzed mobile sales dataset.

---

## 2. Brand Performance

The detailed brand table shows the following sales performance:

| Brand | Transactions | Total Sales |
|---|---:|---:|
| Samsung | 62 | 12,541,805 |
| Apple | 61 | 12,373,380 |
| OnePlus | 60 | 11,603,636 |
| Xiaomi | 57 | ~11.59M |
| Vivo | 51 | 10,660,504 |

### Business Insight:
**Samsung is the leading brand by transaction count and total sales** among the brands shown.

Apple is a very close second, while OnePlus also contributes strongly. Vivo has the lowest transaction count and sales among the five brands displayed.

### Recommendation:
Focus inventory and promotional strategies on **Samsung and Apple**, while analyzing Vivo's comparatively lower performance to identify opportunities for targeted promotions.

---

## 3. Payment Method Analysis

The dashboard shows **291 transactions** distributed across three payment methods:

- Cash: 66 transactions
- UPI: 77 transactions
- Credit Card: 72 transactions
- Remaining transactions are represented by the fourth payment category shown in the visual

### Business Insight:
Digital payment methods represent a significant portion of transactions, with **UPI showing the highest transaction count among the clearly labeled categories**.

### Recommendation:
Continue supporting UPI and card-based payments and consider digital-payment promotions such as cashback or instant discounts to encourage conversions.

---

## 4. Customer Rating Analysis

The rating visual shows:

- **Good:** 88
- **Poor:** 80
- **Average:** 53

These displayed rating counts total **221**, indicating that the rating visual is based on a subset of transactions with available rating information rather than all 291 transactions.

### Business Insight:
Good ratings form the largest group, but the number of Poor ratings is also substantial.

### Recommendation:
Investigate the reasons behind poor customer ratings, particularly around:
- Product quality
- Delivery experience
- Customer service
- Product expectations
- After-sales support

Improving these areas could increase customer satisfaction and repeat purchases.

---

## 5. Monthly Quantity Trend

The monthly quantity chart shows noticeable fluctuations throughout the year.

The strongest visible quantity levels occur around **March and July**, while relatively lower levels appear around **February, April, and September**.

### Business Insight:
Mobile demand is not constant throughout the year, suggesting the presence of seasonal or promotional purchasing patterns.

### Recommendation:
Increase inventory and promotional activity ahead of historically strong demand periods and analyze the reasons behind monthly fluctuations.

---

## 6. Mobile Model Performance

The dashboard highlights models such as:

- Galaxy Note 20
- iPhone 15
- OnePlus Nord

### Business Insight:
The leading models shown have strong sales contribution, indicating that customers are concentrated around popular flagship and mid-to-premium smartphone models.

### Recommendation:
Maintain sufficient stock of high-performing models and use model-level sales trends to improve inventory planning.

---

## 7. City-Level Sales

The geographical map visual shows sales distribution across multiple cities in India and surrounding regions.

### Business Insight:
Sales are geographically distributed across multiple locations rather than being concentrated in a single city.

### Recommendation:
Use city-level sales data to identify high-potential markets and develop localized marketing campaigns, inventory allocation, and distribution strategies.

---

## 8. Daily Sales Performance

The daily sales chart demonstrates significant fluctuations throughout the month, with several visible sales peaks and lower-performing days.

### Business Insight:
Daily sales are uneven, indicating that customer purchasing behavior varies significantly throughout the month.

### Recommendation:
Analyze high-performing days in combination with promotions, weekends, salary cycles, product launches, and marketing campaigns to identify repeatable sales opportunities.

---

# 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

1. **Prioritize high-performing brands**
   - Samsung and Apple show the strongest sales performance.
   - Maintain adequate inventory for these brands.

2. **Improve lower-performing brands**
   - Vivo has comparatively lower transactions and sales.
   - Test targeted offers and promotional campaigns.

3. **Strengthen digital payments**
   - UPI and card payments account for a significant share of transactions.
   - Introduce digital-payment incentives where appropriate.

4. **Improve customer satisfaction**
   - Investigate the causes of the relatively high number of Poor ratings.
   - Strengthen after-sales service and customer support.

5. **Optimize inventory**
   - Use monthly quantity trends to forecast demand.
   - Stock popular models before expected high-demand periods.

6. **Use city-level insights**
   - Identify high-sales cities and allocate inventory and marketing budgets accordingly.

7. **Analyze daily sales patterns**
   - Investigate sales spikes to determine whether promotions, weekends, launches, or other factors drive increased demand.
